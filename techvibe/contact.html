Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 2 to 3 working days. Orders over R2 000 ship free; smaller orders pay a flat R100 delivery fee.

What is your returns policy?

You can return unused products in their original packaging within 30 days for a refund or exchange.

Do products come with a warranty?

Yes. Every product carries a 12-month warranty against manufacturing faults.

Which payment methods do you accept?

This is a demo store, so no real payments are taken. A live shop would accept cards and instant EFT.

Are prices VAT inclusive?

Prices shown are before VAT. 15% VAT is added in your cart and at checkout.
